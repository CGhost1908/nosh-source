PART 2 - PRIVACY NOTICE
-----------------------

Nosh stores your notes locally on your computer. It does not collect
telemetry or usage analytics.

Nosh only connects to other networked systems when you explicitly use a
feature that needs them:

  * Google Drive sync - only after you sign in with your Google account.
    Nosh accesses only the files and folders you choose to use with Nosh.
    You can disconnect your account at any time in the app settings.

  * Public note sharing (Supabase) - only when you choose to publish a note.
    The published note's content is uploaded so that it can be viewed by
    anyone with the link. Notes you do not publish are never uploaded.

  * Embedded web content (e.g. YouTube player) - only when you open it.

If you do not use these features, Nosh does not send your data anywhere.

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/CGhost1908/nosh-source/blob/main/privacy-policy.md

Third-party privacy policies:
  Google:   https://policies.google.com/privacy
  Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy


UNINSTALLING
------------

Nosh can be removed at any time from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps,
or by running "Uninstall Nosh" from the installation folder.
