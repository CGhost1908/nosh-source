Privacy & Security Guarantee
Nosh Privacy & Security Commitment

Nosh is designed on a local-first architecture. Your notes, snippets, tokens, and API credentials are kept strictly private on your device and are never sent to external tracking or analytics servers.

1. Local Storage & Zero Telemetry
All notes are stored on your local disk in standard, readable .nsh format. Nosh contains no telemetry, user tracking, or advertising code.
2. YouTube Player
The YouTube companion player operates strictly via official YouTube embedded frames. Nosh does not collect, log, or track user browsing habits or credentials.
3. Google Drive Sync
Google Drive sync operates via official Google OAuth and accesses only the authorized noshTheNotepad folder. Personal emails and unrelated files are never accessed.
4. Web Publishing (Supabase)
Notes are published to the web only when you explicitly click the "Publish" button. Only the selected note's content is transmitted over HTTPS to generate a shareable link.
